Website redesign: MAC Contracting.

A deck builder and exterior-services business in Canal Winchester, Ohio. I redesigned the homepage to make the services, project examples and estimate process easier for homeowners to understand.

01. A clearer route to an estimate.

The redesigned homepage gives the service and location more emphasis, brings review proof forward, and groups the estimate fields into a clear enquiry area.

02. Services homeowners can compare.

Service photography and explanations help visitors understand design-build, deck replacement and structural repair, with a next step alongside each service.

03. Local information in the right places.

Service-area details, location information and customer reviews become separate sections that homeowners can scan before getting in touch.
